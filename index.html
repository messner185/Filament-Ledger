PRICING
How pricing works & fee sources

All monetary inputs use your selected currency. Dollar examples above are USD, not automatic currency conversions. Labor and margin defaults are illustrative. Fee examples checked October 2, 2026: Stripe pricing, Etsy fees, and Etsy payment processing. Etsy advertising and other charges, international cards, fee bases including shipping/tax, and local rates can change the result. Enter your actual combined fees. Treat each saved print as one order, or allocate fixed fees across its items.

Price = (print cost + labor + overhead + fixed fee) ÷ (1 − selling fee % − profit margin %). Price rounds up to your currency's smallest unit. Saving settings affects new estimates; historical quotes stay saved. Loading a plan uses current rates with its saved labor minutes and overhead.
